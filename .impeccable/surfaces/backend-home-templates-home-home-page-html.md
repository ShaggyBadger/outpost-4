# GrannyCoded Home Surface

## Scope and visitor mode

Persuade. This is the landing page for GrannyCoded’s direct-sale shop, with
paths into the catalog, maker’s blog, and social profiles.

## Audience, job, proof, and constraints

Speak equally to handmade-goods shoppers and people drawn to vintage quilts and
one-of-a-kind wearable pieces. Help them understand the quilt-to-garment craft,
see a real available item, and shop it. Preserve the GrannyCoded logo and
supplied five-color palette. Use verified product names, stock, and imagery;
social links and final maker biography are not yet supplied.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make the found textile’s transformation the shop’s organizing idea; refuse a generic hero followed by an undifferentiated grid.

OWN-WORLD: A thrift-store source tag becomes the catalog’s label language, paired with actual quilt texture and the committed GrannyCoded olive, moss, gold, linen, and ink palette.

STORY: Visitors recognize how a found quilt becomes a one-of-a-kind piece, see one real product with its availability and price, then enter the full shop; blog and social links support that journey.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Place the logo and Shop, Journal, and About navigation in the header. Let one large, real quilt-made garment photograph lead. Overlay a paper source tag that states the quilt-to-piece idea, shows the item’s availability and price, and carries the primary Shop this piece action. Let the next section begin visibly as an index of available one-offs.

FORM: Found Textile: The Source Tag, the top-ranked grounded direction selected over the assigned Textile Sample Book; seed:74f4ad71d211c6c3.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
