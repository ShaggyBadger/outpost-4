# GrannyCoded Storefront

Language for the handmade-goods catalog, sales, and custom-work process.

## Catalog

**Batch listing**:
A listing for several similar finished pieces sold from one shared quantity.

**One-off piece**:
A unique finished item with one available unit. After it sells, it remains
visible as sold.

**Variant**:
A predefined version of a product whose price or available quantity is managed
separately, such as a size with its own stock count.

**Product option**:
A shopper-selected detail recorded with an order that does not have separate
stock or pricing.

**Ready-to-ship item**:
A finished piece that is physically available and can be sold immediately.

## Orders

**Custom inquiry**:
A customer's request for work that is not offered as a ready-to-buy listing.

**Quote**:
The agreed scope, price, and shipping terms for a custom order before payment.

**Custom order**:
A bespoke piece made after the customer accepts the quote and pays the agreed
amount.

**Payment invoice**:
A request for the customer to pay the agreed amount for an accepted custom
order.

**Guest checkout**:
A purchase without a customer account. Shipped online orders still provide
contact and delivery details.

**In-person sale**:
A purchase completed directly with Leah, recorded so the shop's available
inventory stays accurate.

**Fair withdrawal**:
The temporary removal of items from online sale while Leah takes them to an
in-person event. Unsold pieces return to the available catalog afterward.
